Questions
Do I own the clips?
Yes. What you render is yours to ship. Paid plans export without a watermark, and the licence covers commercial use and resale of the finished film, not of the raw model.
How long is a clip?
Eight seconds by default, twelve at most. Extension adds four seconds at a time, and the last frame of one extension becomes the first frame of the next.
Can it hold a face?
Faces drift once you pass three seconds. For dialogue, render an empty plate at the right length and cut the performance in afterwards.
What about my own footage?
Image to video takes a still as its first frame, so the shot begins exactly where your photograph stops moving.
Do the twelve differ?
They are twelve seeds of one prompt. Not variations of a single answer: twelve separate takes, any of which can be extended on its own.
Is there an API?
There is. A render is one POST to /v2/render with your prompt and seed count, and a webhook when the takes come back.
